Send every finished run; only a player's best counts. Scores are whole numbers, times are milliseconds.
Pass the same order and format every time: the first score sets them, and you can reset the board on the game's page.
top() gives up to 100 places with players' names; show them with textContent.
Signed-out players keep their best in the browser.
List it in features as "highscores".
